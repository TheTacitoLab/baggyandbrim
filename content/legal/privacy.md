## 2. What this notice covers

This notice explains what we do with personal information when you make an enquiry through our website, when you place an order with us, and when you browse the site. It also explains what happens to information about other people that you send us, such as the names and sizes of your players.

## 3. Information we collect

### Information you give us

When you complete an enquiry form we collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your telephone number, if you provide one
- The name of your club, school, team or organisation
- The products you are interested in
- Your approximate order volume
- Anything else you write in your message

When you go on to order from us we also collect:

- Delivery and invoicing addresses
- Billing contact details
- Artwork you send us, such as club crests, sponsor logos and design references
- Sizing and personalisation details for the people the items are for
- Correspondence between us

### Information about other people

Cricket kit is personalised. To make it, we usually need the names, initials, squad numbers and sizes of the people who will wear it. Some of those people will be children.

**Where you send us information about other people, you are responsible for it.** You must have a lawful basis for sharing it with us and, where the law requires it, you must have told those people, or their parents or guardians, that their details are being passed to a supplier to have kit made. We use that information only to manufacture and deliver your order, and we delete it in line with section 8.

If you would rather not send us player names at all, tell us and we will work from a numbered list instead.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit the site we collect limited technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you view and the site you arrived from. See section 10 on cookies.

### Information we do not collect

We do not take payment through this website, so we do not collect or store card details. Payment is arranged separately once an order is agreed.

## 4. Why we use your information, and our lawful basis

| What we do | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and answer questions | Legitimate interests: responding to someone who has approached us about our products |
| Produce artwork, take your approval and manufacture your order | Performance of a contract with you, or steps taken at your request before entering one |
| Arrange delivery | Performance of a contract |
| Keep records of orders, invoices and approvals | Legal obligation for tax and accounting records, and legitimate interests in being able to evidence what was agreed |
| Handle a complaint, defect claim or dispute | Legitimate interests in defending our position and resolving the matter |
| Send you occasional emails about our products, if you have asked for them | Consent, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Keep the site secure and prevent spam and fraudulent submissions | Legitimate interests in protecting our systems |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have considered whether our interests are overridden by your rights, and we have concluded that they are not because the processing is limited to what is needed to respond to you and to run the order you have asked for.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

## 5. Enquiries are not orders

Sending an enquiry form does not place an order and does not commit you to anything. We use it to understand what you need and to send you a quotation. An order only exists once you have approved a design in writing, as set out in our Terms.

## 6. Who we share your information with

We share personal information with the following categories of recipient:

- **Web3Forms**, operated by Web3Creative, which processes our enquiry form submissions and forwards them to our inbox. Web3Forms acts as our processor under a data processing agreement. Its infrastructure is hosted on Amazon Web Services with Cloudflare and Hetzner as further infrastructure providers, and its servers are in the United States.
- **Our email and hosting providers**, who process information in the course of storing and delivering our correspondence and running the website.
- **Our manufacturing partners**, who receive the specification for your order, including artwork and any names, numbers and sizes needed to make the items. They receive only what is needed to produce your order.
- **Our delivery partners**, who receive the delivery address and a contact name and number.
- **Our accountants, insurers and professional advisers**, where required.
- **Regulators, law enforcement or other authorities**, where we are legally required to disclose.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## 7. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers are outside the UK, including Web3Forms, whose servers are in the United States. Some of our manufacturing partners are outside the UK.

Where personal information is transferred outside the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards, which will be one of the following: a UK adequacy decision covering the destination country, the International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

You can ask us for details of the safeguards applying to a specific transfer by emailing hello@baggyandbrim.com.

| Information | Retention |

| Marketing contacts | Until you withdraw consent, or after 24 months of no engagement |

## 9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
- Ask us to delete information, where there is no continuing reason for us to hold it
- Ask us to restrict how we use your information while a query is resolved
- Object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interests
- Ask us to transfer information you gave us to another organisation, where the processing is by consent or under a contract and carried out by automated means
- Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent
- Object to direct marketing at any time, absolutely

To exercise any of these, email hello@baggyandbrim.com. We will respond within one month. There is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or by calling 0303 123 1113. We would ask you to raise it with us first so we have the chance to put it right.

## 11. Keeping information secure

We use HTTPS across the site, restrict access to enquiry and order information to those who need it, and choose providers who encrypt data at rest and in transit. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information you send us before it reaches us.

If a breach occurs that is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours and tell you where the law requires it.
